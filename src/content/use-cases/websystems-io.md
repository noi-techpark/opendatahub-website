Knowing where parking spaces are available can help them decide where to leave their car and how to continue using other forms of transport.</p> <b><a href='https://websystems.io/en/'>Websystems.io</a></b> <b>integrates parking data provided through the Open Data Hub</b> into two of its mobility platforms, <b><a href='https://www.bikemeran.it/en/info/parking/'>BikeMeran.it</a></b> and <b><a href='https://www.fahrplan.it/en/park-and-ride/'>Fahrplan.it</a></b>. Fahrplan.it, launched in 2024, has been using the same data since 2026 in a park-and-ride context, combining parking information with public transport options.<br><br><b>Together, the two platforms show how the same reliable mobility data can be reused across different services and mobility needs.</b>"

paragraphs:

  - description: "Websystems.io integrates data from the **Open Data Hub Mobility API** into its mobility platforms. The **Parking Station Dataset** provides real-time occupancy information for parking stations in and around Merano, including Park Huber, Park Karl Wolf, Park Kellerei Algund, Park Plaza and Park Terme.<br><br>This dataset gives access to current parking occupancy information without requiring them to create and maintain their own separate data source.<br><br>For Websystems.io, the value lies not only in displaying parking availability, but also in combining this information with other mobility options. On BikeMeran.it, parking becomes part of a journey that can continue by bicycle. On Fahrplan.it, the information supports a park-and-ride approach in which travellers can switch from their car to public transport.<br><br>In this way, **real-time parking data becomes a building block for broader mobility services**."
